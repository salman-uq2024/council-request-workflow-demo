| Request ID | Type | Status | Why it matters |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

## Why include these records

- Reviewers can understand the workflow without entering data first.
- The dashboard opens with a mix of statuses instead of a blank state.
- The audit screen shows realistic activity across multiple request types.
- The project presents better in a hiring context because the sample data reflects genuine business scenarios rather than placeholder lorem ipsum content.
